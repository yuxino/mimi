## 修复与改进

- 修复采集失败或字幕未启动时，浮层仍显示“正在聆听”的问题。
- 为错误和未启动状态显示明确的中英日文提示，停止对应的聆听动画。
- 补充 macOS 录制权限已开启但采集仍被拒绝时，针对 Mimi 单条授权记录的恢复步骤。

## Fixes and improvements

- Stop reporting Listening when capture has failed or subtitles have not started.
- Show explicit error and idle labels in Chinese, English, and Japanese, with a static status indicator.
- Document a single-app recovery procedure for macOS recording access that remains denied despite an enabled permission switch.
