## English

### What's changed

- Redesign Settings with a persistent sidebar, clearer typography, and lighter section dividers. A compact session control keeps start/stop and status within reach.
- Give **Session export** its own page, with clearer retention switches, export actions, and expandable details about limits and timestamps.
- Add visual previews for **System / Light / Dark** appearance and improve narrow-window layouts in English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.
- Preserve ongoing update and export operations when navigating between settings pages, and keep export controls accessible to assistive technology.

The two session-retention switches remain **off by default**. Existing choices are preserved; content stays in memory until you manually export it. Turning a switch off clears its buffer; starting a new session or quitting clears both. System-audio capture and translation behavior are unchanged.

### Updating and verification

Open **Settings → General → Software Update**, or download the installer below. Export controls are now under **Settings → Session export**.

The macOS package is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; Gatekeeper, Screen & System Audio Recording, or Keychain approval may be required again. Windows packages are not Authenticode-signed, so SmartScreen may warn. Updater signatures verify integrity and do not replace platform code signing.

Verification covers the Rust/frontend checks and native macOS development-app UI checks across all four settings pages, both themes, narrow windows, and all three interface languages. Session/export controls were checked with synthetic content in UI-only mode. Windows device interaction, real-provider recording, and complete in-place updater acceptance remain unverified.

## 中文

### 本次改进

- 重新设计设置界面：固定侧边导航，梳理文字层级，使用更轻的分隔线。精简后的会话控制区让启停操作和运行状态始终可见。
- 将「**会话导出**」独立为一个页面，清晰区分暂存开关、导出操作，并可展开查看容量上限和时间戳说明。
- 为「**跟随系统／浅色／深色**」增加外观预览，改善窄窗口下的布局，同步支持英文、简体中文和日文。
- 切换设置页面时保留正在进行的更新和导出操作，并确保辅助技术能够访问导出控件。

两项会话暂存开关仍然**默认关闭**，已有选择会保留。内容仅暂存在内存中，手动导出才写入文件。关闭某个开关会清空对应内容；开始新会话或退出应用会清空两项内容。系统音频采集和翻译行为保持不变。

### 更新与验证说明

在「**设置 → 通用 → 软件更新**」中更新，或下载下方安装包。导出功能现位于「**设置 → 会话导出**」。

macOS 包为 ad-hoc 签名，未经 Apple 公证，可能需要重新允许 Gatekeeper、屏幕与系统音频录制或钥匙串访问。Windows 包未做 Authenticode 签名，SmartScreen 可能提示警告。更新签名验证完整性，不替代平台代码签名。

验证包括 Rust／前端检查，以及 macOS 原生开发版的四个设置页面、深浅主题、窄窗口和三种界面语言。会话与导出控件使用纯 UI 测试模式下的合成内容检查。Windows 实机交互、真实服务商录音和完整原地更新仍未验证。
